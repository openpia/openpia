# OpenPIA Charter

## Mission

To remove the operational friction that Altnets, build partners, and their field teams hit every day in the Openreach PIA process — by creating an open, royalty-free standard for the A55a/A55b workflow that captures the operational reality proprietary tools leave out, and gets the right evidence captured first time.

## Principles

1. **Open & royalty-free.** Freely usable by anyone, forever. The standard is a shared industry asset, never a product sold back to the people who built it.
2. **Built from the field up.** Shaped by the people who plan, survey, and build — not imposed top-down. The van's-eye view sits alongside the planning office.
3. **Workflow-first.** Models the operational reality — job types, points, stages, blockages, and the evidence at each — not just the moment of submission.
4. **Evidence, done right.** Explicit evidence slots (capture prompts) so the correct photos are taken against a known requirement, rather than guessed at after the fact.
5. **Vendor-neutral.** Defines the interchange, not the product. It complements existing tools and lowers the cost of integrating between them.
6. **Open governance.** Decisions are made in public, versioned, and always open to challenge. Nothing is settled behind a closed door.

## Scope

**In scope:** the **A55a** (point-based reactive works) and **A55b** (stage-based civil evidence) notice schemas; the job-type and infrastructure taxonomies; the **evidence taxonomy** (required slot codes) and the A55a `job_type × infrastructure_category × point_type` required-slot matrix; field blockage / no-build capture; and the **validation rules** that define a complete, compliant submission.

**Out of scope:** replacing operators' internal systems; commercial tooling; user interfaces; pricing; machine-learning / scoring; and the internal APIs of any submission portal. OpenPIA describes *how data is structured and exchanged*, not how any one organisation runs its operation.
